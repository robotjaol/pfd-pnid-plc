# Dataset Policy and Cards

External data are not committed unless redistribution rights are explicit. Download from the authoritative source, record the dataset version and checksum, and preserve equipment or scenario groups during evaluation.

## ESPset

Real vibration measurements for ESP fault diagnosis. Use the experimental framework and equipment-aware partitions supplied by the authors. Source: <https://github.com/NINFA-UFES/ESPset>. Paper DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2024.111452>.

## Petrobras 3W

Multivariate time series containing undesirable events in oil wells from real, simulated, and hand-drawn sources. Source: <https://github.com/petrobras/3W>. Version 2.0 paper DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-026-07225-z>.

## Synthetic OpenLift data

Generated from versioned scenarios in this repository. Suitable for software integration and controlled fault injection, but not evidence of field generalization.
