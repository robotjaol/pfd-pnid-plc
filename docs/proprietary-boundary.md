# Patent and Proprietary Boundary

This repository is independently developed from public sources. References to SLB Intelligent Lift, Lift IQ, Lift Operations Advisor, or other vendor offerings describe publicly advertised problem classes and industry direction only.

The repository does not include vendor software, reverse-engineered protocols, confidential operational data, proprietary pump curves, trade secrets, trademarks as branding, or claims of functional equivalence. Contributors must document the origin and license of all third-party data and must not upload employer or customer information.

A patent landscape is not a freedom-to-operate opinion. Before commercialization, obtain a qualified intellectual-property review and a separate process-safety, electrical, cybersecurity, and regulatory assessment.
