# HMI Screen Philosophy

The HMI supports overview, interactive P&ID, well and ESP status, IPR/VLP and pump curves, VFD control, trends, energy, alarm summary, first-out, recommendation and confidence, fault injection, replay, controller comparison, health, and engineering settings.

Commands require mode visibility, feedback, permissive status and confirmation for consequential actions. Alarm priority, shelving, deadband, delay, first-out and acknowledgement must be implemented deliberately. Analytics are visually separated from deterministic protection.
