# Parametric CAD Scope

## Assemblies

1. Simplified downhole string: motor, protector, intake, gas separator, pump, gauge and cable clamps.
2. Surface package: wellhead, VFD cabinet, control cabinet and junction box.
3. Edge I/O enclosure: DIN-rail carrier, terminal clearance, glands and service access.

## Required exports

Native FreeCAD document, STEP assembly, general arrangement, exploded view, interface-control drawing, mass-properties report and interference report. Dimensions remain configurable placeholders until vendor-independent reference requirements are approved.

## Definition of done

No unresolved interference, consistent coordinate system, named interfaces, revision block, parameter table, BOM link and generated previews. Decorative 3D models do not satisfy the work package.
