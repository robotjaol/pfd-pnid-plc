# Process Basis of Design

The reference process is one onshore producer with reservoir inflow, completion, downhole gauge, ESP motor, protector, gas separator, multistage pump, production tubing, wellhead, surface choke and export boundary. The virtual plant is designed for surveillance and control experiments, not flow assurance or equipment sizing.

## Normal path

Reservoir -> completion -> ESP intake -> ESP discharge -> tubing -> wellhead -> production choke -> export boundary.

## Study disturbances

Reservoir decline, blockage, gas interference, efficiency degradation, temperature rise, sensor bias, drift, freeze, dropout, communication delay, packet loss and actuator nonresponse.
