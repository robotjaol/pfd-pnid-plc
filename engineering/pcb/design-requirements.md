# OpenLift Edge I/O Interface Board

The board is a digital design exercise for four isolated 4-20 mA inputs, two 0-10 V inputs, isolated 24 V digital inputs, protected outputs, isolated RS-485, USB debugging, watchdog, reverse-polarity protection, surge protection and a replaceable ESP32 or STM32 processing module.

## Verification gates

1. Requirement-to-schematic traceability.
2. ERC with no unresolved critical findings.
3. DRC with no unresolved critical findings.
4. Creepage, clearance and isolation review against the selected voltage and environment.
5. Analog error budget and thermal review.
6. Interface simulation and Modbus conformance test.
7. Schematic, PCB, BOM, Gerber, position file and 3D export under revision control.

The design must not be described as intrinsically safe or hazardous-area compliant without independent certification.
