# Electrical Design Basis

Conceptual power path: source, switchgear, VFD, step-up transformer, ESP cable, and motor. Control power uses a segregated 24 VDC supply for PLC, HMI, communications, virtual instrument interfaces, and the edge I/O board.

Deliverables planned for QElectroTech include SLD, control schematic, terminal plan, interconnection diagram, cable schedule, panel layout and earthing concept. All ratings in this repository are illustrative until a real equipment datasheet, short-circuit study, hazardous-area classification, cable calculation, protection-coordination study and applicable code review are supplied.
